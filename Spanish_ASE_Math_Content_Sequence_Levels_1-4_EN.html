Spanish ASE Math: Content Sequence by Level (Levels 1–4)

City Colleges of Chicago — Adult Education · Levels 1–4 · 96 hours per level

Standard Numbers correspond to the Illinois ABE/ASE Mathematics Content Standards (ICCB, rev. February 2021).

Level-to-NRS correspondence:
Nivel 1 = NRS 1–2 | Nivel 2 = NRS 2–3 | Nivel 3 = NRS 3–4 | Nivel 4 = NRS 4–5 (GED) .

The Spanish ASE Math program condenses the six NRS levels into four 96-hour levels, with some content moving down and some moving up to fit the four authorized terms. Each topic below is an accordion; within it, every subtopic shows a four-level table. An empty cell means the content is not addressed at that level, so the full row reveals the learning sequence. Each cell lists its corresponding Standard Number.
